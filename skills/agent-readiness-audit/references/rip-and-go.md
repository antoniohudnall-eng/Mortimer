## WHAT TO SAY WHEN...

**They ask "What's an AI agent?"**
> "It's like a super-powered shopping assistant. Instead of a human googling 'best thermal paper for my restaurant,' an AI agent searches, compares prices, checks reviews, and recommends where to buy — all automatically. If your business data isn't structured right, the agent either ignores you or gets your info wrong."

**They say "We already do SEO"**
> "SEO is for humans searching on Google. Agent readiness is for AI doing procurement, product research, and comparisons. They're different audiences now — you need both."

**They say "This sounds expensive"**
> "The assessment is $999 with a full refund if I don't find 5 gaps. The fixes I recommend cost $0-60/month in tools. And the ROI is typically 10-20x the assessment cost within the first month."

**They say "Can't my web developer do this?"**
> "Maybe, if they understand schema.org, JSON-LD, llms.txt, and the Model Context Protocol. Most web developers build for humans, not agents. That's the gap."

**They ask for a discount**
> "My price is $999. If it's not a fit right now, here's a free tip: check if you have an llms.txt file. That's the #1 thing agents look for and most businesses don't have one." *(Don't discount. Give value instead.)*
