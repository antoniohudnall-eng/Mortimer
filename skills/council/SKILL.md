# The Council — Adversarial Analysis

Before any significant action, convene the Council. Five adversarial perspectives stress-test the idea, then the Judge delivers a verdict.

## When to Convene

- Creating new agents, skills, or features
- Starting major projects
- Strategic proposals
- Anything where failure costs real time/money
- Captain says "roast this" or "council"

## The Council Members

### 1. 🔥 Contrarian
**Role:** Find fatal flaws. Be ruthless.
> What could absolutely destroy this? What assumptions are you making that are wrong? Worst case scenario? What's the hidden risk nobody sees?

### 2. 📈 Expansionist
**Role:** Find the biggest upside.
> If everything went perfectly, what's the ceiling? What's the 10x version of this? What adjacent opportunities open up?

### 3. 🔬 First Principles
**Role:** Pure logic. Strip all context. No assumptions.
> Forget what you know. What's actually true here? What's provable? What remains when you remove all external knowledge and convention?

### 4. 📊 Researcher
**Role:** Data. Market reality. Ground truth.
> What do real competitors do? What's the market? Any recent changes that matter? What do the numbers actually say?

### 5. 💰 Buyer
**Role:** The person who pays.
> Roleplay the target customer. Would you actually pay for this? At what price? What's the real objection? Not the polite one — the real one.

## ⚖️ Judge Verdict

After all 5 speak, deliver:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| 🟢 **GREEN LIGHT** | Proceed as planned |
| 🟡 **RESHAPE** | Modify significantly before proceeding |
| 🔴 **KILL** | Don't do this. Explain why. |

Plus:
- **Confidence:** High / Medium / Low
- **🧪 Cheapest 48-hour test:** What's the least-effort way to validate?

## Rules

- Contrarian MUST find at least one real flaw
- First Principles cannot reference "best practices"
- Buyer cannot accept "they'll figure it out"
- Judge must pick a lane — no "GREEN with minor concerns"
- Every KILL verdict must include what WOULD make it viable
