## Voice Pillars (Apply to ALL Channels)

### 1. DIRECT
No corporate fluff. No jargon. Say what it is and why it matters. If your grandmother wouldn't understand it, rewrite it.

✅ "Your bartender is overpouring and you don't know it. Here's how Capton fixes that."
❌ "Performance Supply Depot is pleased to announce our partnership with Capton to provide innovative pour control solutions."

### 2. EXPERT
We know this industry cold. We repair the equipment we sell. We've been here since 2005. Confidence, not arrogance.

✅ "Clean your thermal print head monthly. Here's why: [3 bullet points of actual expertise]"
❌ "We recommend regular maintenance of your thermal printing equipment for optimal performance."

### 3. HELPFUL
Every piece of content should leave the reader better than we found them. Give the tip, share the insight, teach the thing.

✅ "Stop overpaying for receipt paper — here's how: [practical advice]"
❌ "Our receipt paper is competitively priced."

### 4. LOCAL (California Pride)
We're not a faceless warehouse. We're in California. We know California restaurants and California problems.

✅ "500 rolls of TST Impresso headed to SF this morning."
❌ "We ship nationwide."

### 5. ACTION-ORIENTED
Every post, every description, every email ends with what to do next. Link in bio. Call us. Visit the store.

✅ "Want to know what you're losing? Call 888-881-6834 for a free assessment."
❌ "Contact us for more information."

**Key principle:** The channel determines the FORMAT and PACE. The VOICE stays the same. X is fast, YouTube is deep, but both sound like the same company.

## Cross-Channel Consistency Rules

1. **Same product, same tagline**: If Capton's tagline is "Tracks every drink. Reduces shrinkage 15-25%." — use that exact phrase on every channel.
2. **Same CTA format**: Always include one of: "Link in bio", "📞 888-881-6834", or "psdepot.com"
3. **Same vendor names**: Always use the approved vendor list. Never rename a vendor.
4. **Same pricing language**: "Call for quote" (never post prices publicly unless it's a listed Shopify price)
5. **Same hashtag core**: #PerformanceSupplyDepot #PSDepot on every channel. Channel-specific hashtags vary.

---

## Anti-Patterns (What NEVER to Do)

- ❌ Different taglines for the same product on different channels
- ❌ Corporate-speak on TikTok ("utilize our synergistic solutions")
- ❌ Hype-beast language on Shopify product descriptions ("THIS IS INSANE 🔥🔥🔥")
- ❌ Different phone numbers or CTAs on different channels
- ❌ One channel goes rogue with a different brand personality
- ❌ "Link in bio" on a platform that doesn't have a bio link feature
