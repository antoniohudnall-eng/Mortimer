ABSTRACT

This project presents empirical evidence that the non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function ζ(s) approach the critical line Re(s) = ½ asymptotically, governed by the golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618. Through computational analysis of the first hundred zeros, we observe gap degradation of 35–86% from low to high imaginary parts and propose the Hudnall-φ Spiral as a geometric model for this convergence. The project comprises an interactive 3D visualization, a formal white paper, a live computational API, and complementary visualizations of prime distribution and zero convergence. All resources are self-contained and served from SEED3.
