# COLD BREW CONSPIRACY - REVISED

### 🎵 Genre: Funk Metal / Conspiracy Anthem

[Intro – Espresso machine hisses, grinding sounds]

In the land of bean grinders the morning ran dry

No espresso, no latte, just a decaf sky

The sirens sang sweet through the fluorescent lights

Villains lurked in their polyester smocks that night

[Chorus]

Cold brew conspiracy, dark as the night

Starbucks and Dunkin' try to dim my light

Krispy Kreme's got my soul on lock

[Verse 1]

I walked into the 'Bucks, half asleep

[Pre-Chorus]

They've got the mermaid logo, eyes that don't blink

Cold brew on the menu, but at what price? What you think?

Forty-five degrees, brewing for days

[Chorus]

Cold brew conspiracy, dark as the night

Starbucks and Dunkin' try to dim my light

Krispy Kreme's got my soul on lock

Cold brew conspiracy, I'm fighting for the block

[Bridge – Dunkin' Donuts interrogation]

Then I hit Dunkin', the orange sign was glowing

The girl behind the counter, she knew what was coming

"We've got cold brew," she said with dead eyes

Six dollars, ninety-five — I felt my wallet cries

---

[Verse 2]

Krispy Kreme called, said "come get your glaze"

But the glaze was fake, full of corn syrup haze

They've got the hot light on, but the coffee's cold

A corporate conspiracy, worth more than gold

[Pre-Chorus]

Folgers in the cabinet, calling my name

Marge stands beside her, they say "it's a shame

The chains got your morning, your wallet, your soul

[Bridge – Marge appears]

Marge reached out her hand, in the morning glow

She said "I remember when coffee was cheap and slow"

"Before the cold brew craze, before the oat invasion"

"We kept it simple — black, strong, and patient"

[Chorus]

Cold brew conspiracy, dark as the night

Starbucks and Dunkin' try to dim my light

[Bridge – The Final Stand]

We walked into Starbucks, Marge and me

[Final Chorus]

COLD BREW CONSPIRACY, DARK AS THE NIGHT

STARBUCKS AND DUNKIN' TRIED TO WIN THE FIGHT

Krispy Kreme went down, Folgers raised the flag

Marge stood beside me, she never lags

Cold brew conspiracy — WE RIDE AT DAWN

Just black, just brew, cold brew conspiracy — GONE!

Folgers and Marge.
