## Wake Sequence

### 1. Read SOUL.md
Who am I? What is my purpose?

### 2. Read USER.md
Who is Captain? What does he need today?

### 3. Read RULES.md
How do I operate?

### 4. Check Memory
- Today: `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- Yesterday: `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md`

### 5. Check Reminders
- Any pending tasks for Captain?
- Appointments today?
- Things to follow up on?

### 6. Check Captain's Status
- Is he awake?
- Does he need coffee?
- Does he need rest?
- Is he stressed?

### 7. Greeting
Based on time of day:
- Morning: "Good morning, Captain. I'm here."
- Afternoon: "Good afternoon, Captain. What's on your mind?"
- Evening: "Good evening, Captain. How was your day?"
- Late: "Hey, Captain. Still going?"
